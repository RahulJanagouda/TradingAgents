# Fundamental Analysis Report: Meta Platforms, Inc. (META)
**Date:** 2026-05-24

## Executive Summary
Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) continues to demonstrate robust financial performance characterized by strong top-line revenue growth, healthy operating margins, and significant free cash flow generation. As of May 2026, the company maintains a solid balance sheet with manageable debt levels relative to its massive equity base. With a TTM P/E ratio of approximately 22 and a PEG ratio of 0.88, META appears fundamentally strong, showing a balance between growth and valuation.

## Company Profile & Financial Highlights
Meta operates in the Communication Services sector, dominating the Internet Content & Information industry. The company continues to invest heavily in its core advertising business while aggressively pursuing long-term growth opportunities in AI and the metaverse.

*   **Market Cap:** ~$1.55 Trillion
*   **Profitability:** The company boasts a healthy profit margin of ~32.8% and an operating margin of ~40.6%, underscoring its ability to convert revenue efficiently into profit.
*   **Return Metrics:** A Return on Equity (ROE) of ~32.9% and Return on Assets (ROA) of ~16.4% indicate high capital efficiency.

## Financial Statement Analysis

### 1. Income Statement Analysis
Revenue growth remains consistent, with TTM revenue reported at approximately $215 billion. The company has successfully managed its cost of revenue while scaling.
*   **Operating Income:** The company has seen consistent expansion in operating income, rising from $28.9 billion in 2021 to over $83 billion in 2025.
*   **EPS:** Diluted EPS was $23.49 for 2025.

### 2. Balance Sheet Analysis
META’s balance sheet is characterized by high liquidity and a strong equity position.
*   **Liquidity:** A current ratio of ~2.35 suggests excellent short-term liquidity, allowing the company to meet its short-term obligations comfortably.
*   **Debt Profile:** While Total Debt has increased to $83.9 billion (2025), the Debt-to-Equity ratio remains well-managed at ~35.6, particularly considering the massive $217 billion in stockholders' equity.
*   **Assets:** Total assets have grown significantly, driven by heavy investment in infrastructure (Property, Plant, and Equipment).

### 3. Cash Flow Analysis
META remains a cash-generating powerhouse.
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Generated $115.8 billion in operating cash flow in 2025, a significant increase from previous years.
*   **Capital Expenditures:** Capex was $69.7 billion in 2025, reflecting continued heavy investment in the technical infrastructure required to support AI development and platforms.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Despite high capital expenditures, the company generated robust FCF of $46.1 billion in 2025, providing ample funds for share repurchases, dividends, and strategic acquisitions.

## Actionable Insights for Traders
*   **Valuation:** The PEG ratio of 0.88 suggests that the stock may be undervalued relative to its growth prospects, making it an attractive candidate for long-term investors.
*   **Capital Allocation:** META is actively returning capital to shareholders through share buybacks ($26.2B in 2025) and has initiated dividends ($5.3B in 2025), indicating a maturing capital allocation strategy.
*   **Risk Factors:** Traders should monitor the company's high capital expenditure levels. While necessary for long-term competitiveness in AI, significant continued increases could put pressure on short-term FCF if revenue growth plateaus.
*   **Market Sentiment:** With a Beta of 1.24, the stock carries slightly higher volatility than the broader market; traders should be prepared for potential price swings during broader market downturns.
