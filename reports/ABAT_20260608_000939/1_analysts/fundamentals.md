# Fundamental Analysis Report: American Battery Technology Company (ABAT)

**Date of Analysis:** 2026-06-08
**Ticker:** ABAT
**Sector:** Industrials
**Industry:** Waste Management (Focused on battery recycling/processing)

## 1. Company Overview
American Battery Technology Company (ABAT) operates in the industrial sector, focusing on the processing and recycling of battery materials. The company's business model is centered on the growing need for sustainable battery material supply chains. As an emerging growth company, ABAT is currently in a capital-intensive phase, characterized by significant investment in facilities and technology, which has not yet translated into profitability.

## 2. Financial Analysis

### Income Statement & Profitability
ABAT is currently generating substantial net losses. With a trailing twelve-month (TTM) net income of approximately **-$63.6 million** and a negative profit margin, the company is deeply unprofitable. Revenue for the TTM period stands at **$16.3 million**, which is insufficient to cover the company's operating costs, leading to negative EBITDA. The high levels of Research & Development and Selling, General, and Administrative expenses reflect its stage as an early-stage company investing heavily to establish operations.

### Balance Sheet & Liquidity
The balance sheet as of 2026-03-31 shows total assets of **$119.4 million** and total liabilities of **$6.7 million**. While the company maintains a high current ratio (**8.12**), indicating strong short-term liquidity, its cash position has been declining. As of the most recent quarterly report, cash and cash equivalents stood at **$37.7 million**, down from higher levels in previous quarters. The company's growth is heavily funded by the issuance of common stock (equity financing), which has led to significant share dilution for existing shareholders.

### Cash Flow
The company's cash flow statement highlights a classic scenario for a high-growth, early-stage company: massive cash burn.
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Consistently negative.
*   **Investing Cash Flow:** Also negative, reflecting continued heavy investment in Property, Plant, and Equipment (CAPEX).
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Strongly negative.
The company is funding this cash burn primarily through financing activities, specifically the issuance of capital stock.

## 3. Actionable Insights & Risks

### Key Risks
1.  **Persistent Financial Losses:** There is no evidence of path to profitability in the near term. The company continues to burn cash at an unsustainable rate.
2.  **Shareholder Dilution:** To fund its operations, ABAT has frequently issued new shares, which dilutes existing shareholders. This is a significant risk for long-term investors.
3.  **Capital Intensive Nature:** The battery recycling industry requires massive CAPEX. If ABAT fails to achieve operational efficiency or market adoption, it may run out of capital, requiring further dilutive financing or debt, which would increase financial risk.
4.  **Market Speculation:** Given its financial profile, the stock's price is likely driven by market sentiment and speculation regarding the green energy transition rather than fundamental valuation.

### Actionable Insight
Traders should exercise extreme caution. ABAT is a highly speculative, high-risk play. The fundamentals do not currently support a bullish thesis. Those who are long on the stock are betting entirely on future success, despite current severe cash burn and dilution. For most investors, the risk-to-reward ratio is unfavorable.
