### 2. The Macroeconomic Reality Check
You claim that rising energy costs create a "moat" for recycling. That is a fundamental misunderstanding of industrial economics. 

Recycling facilities are energy-intensive. When oil, gas, and electricity prices rise, the cost to run those facilities skyrockets. It doesn't magically make the "urban ore" cheaper to process. Furthermore, we are in a high-interest-rate environment where the cost of capital is expensive. A company with negative free cash flow, $37.7M in cash that is rapidly dwindling, and zero profitability is the *last* type of entity you want to hold when capital is tight. You call it a "tailwind"; I call it a liquidity crunch waiting to happen.
